## The question behind the order

A school pays for three things it feels every day: money collected without chasing, parents who answer, and teachers who
save time. A feature that touches none of the three in month one is not high, however complete the catalogue line says
it should be. The Family app is the parent's side of all three, and it now exists (W19), so the high tier is mostly the
school side reaching the family side.
