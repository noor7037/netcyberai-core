# 05 — Money: what the family app can earn without hurting the school business

## 1. Rules (they outrank any pricing idea)

1. **A family never pays for its own school's data or services.** Attendance, fees, receipts, report cards, notices,
   PTM, homework hand-in and study material are the school's service, paid by the school through its NetCyberAI plan.
   Charging the family too would bill twice for one thing and break trust. The registry enforces it (`03` §2).
2. **The school's price never depends on families buying anything.**
3. **No school sees a family's own learning data** (practice, doubts, plan, vault) unless the family shares it.
4. **No advertising, no data sales, no loans, no sales calls** (`02` §6).
5. **The free core stays free.** Plus adds depth, never takes back what was free.

## 2. Revenue models assessed

| Model | Fit | Conflict with the school business | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Family Plus** (subscription, one price for all the family's children) | Direct value: more doubt help, more practice, revision mode, vault export, later voice | None if rule 1 holds | **Go after the pilot shows retention** |
| **School-sponsored Plus** (a school buys Plus for all its families as an add-on to its plan, per student per year) | Schools already pay for outcomes; families get Plus free | Strengthens it: a reason to choose and keep NetCyberAI | **Go**, priced with the school plans |
| **"Invite your school"** (a standalone family asks its school to join) | Distribution for the school system at no acquisition cost | Strengthens it | **Go:** counts per school only, a family's contact shared only with its explicit opt-in |
| Scholarships and exams list | Public good, trust builder | None | **Free always** |
| Advertising | | | **Rejected**: DPDP section 9(3), trust |
| Coaching or course commissions | Money for recommendations | Conflicts with neutral advice | **Rejected for now** |
| Live tutor marketplace | | Safety and operations | **Deferred** |
| Data of any kind | | Illegal and ends trust | **Never** |

## 3. Free and Plus

| Free (every family) | Plus |
|---|---|
| Children and switcher; syllabus from the catalog; reviewed explainers | Doubt help up to a fair-use limit instead of the free daily few |
| Daily practice (a set a day) | Extra practice sets and a full revision mode before exams |
| Revision plan, exam-day kit, weekly summary | Several exam plans at once; vault export as a PDF |
| Scholarships and exams | Voice answers (later) |
| **Every school module for connected children** | |

## 4. Price: a hypothesis to test, not a decision

- **Anchors** (`01` E10): ₹300 a month buys a full video-course plan (PhysicsWallah); the average urban family spends
  ₹3,988 a year per child on coaching (`01` E3).
- **Hypothesis:** a family price well under the video-course anchor, monthly and yearly, tested in two bands at
  pilot schools.
- **Decided only after:** 30-day retention of free users and answers to question 5 of `01` §4. No price appears in the
  product or on the website before that decision (no invented numbers).

## 5. Unit economics

Cost per active child per month =
- AI for doubts (metered, capped per plan);
- plus the AI for library generation, spread over every family (per topic, not per child);
- plus email and messages;
- plus a small share of hosting.

Plus pays when `price × paying share ≥ cost of a free child × free children + cost of a Plus child × Plus children`.

- **[GAP: the chosen provider's price per million tokens]:** the cost per doubt is computed when the founder chooses the
  provider (gate L3). Until then no price is set.
- Caps are set in Control so a month's AI cost can never exceed the platform cap the founder sets.
