The topics are the first numbered items under each chapter ((i) …, (a) …, or A–D in "Study of Compounds"), in the words printed in upright type; the bullets and the explanation under an item are not taken. Good to know:

- The statement printed before (a) in "Chemical Bonding" is not a topic; a colon at the end of an item is dropped ("Electrovalent bonding:").
- Analytical Chemistry (i) is longer than a title may be (300 characters), so the topic is its words before the first colon. Electrolysis (iv) ends "illustrated by the electrolysis of": the list that follows it is not taken.
- The heading "7.Metallurgy" is printed without a space; the chapter is "Metallurgy".
