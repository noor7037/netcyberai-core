The course structure table (page 7) divides the paper into two halves of 40 marks: "MICROECONOMIC THEORY", with its seven parts, and "MACROECONOMICS", with five units. The halves are the units and the table's parts and units are the chapters (the rule of the CBSE file). The topics are the numbered items printed under a chapter in the text: under Money and Banking, Public Finance and National Income.

Good to know:

- <b>Please check:</b> the table numbers MICROECONOMIC THEORY as unit 1 and the macroeconomics units 2–6; here the two halves are the units, their marks left out of the name.
- The table's names are used where the text words them differently: "Concepts of Production" (text: "Concept of production and production function"), "Balance of Payments and Exchange Rate" (text: "Balance of Payment …").
- The microeconomics chapters print their content as running text with an explanation, so they have no topics. One topic of National Income is printed across a page break (pages 9–10).
- Project work is not in the list.
