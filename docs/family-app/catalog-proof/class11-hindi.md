No picture of CBSE's or NCERT's pages is kept in the repository (the documents are theirs, and NCERT prints "© NCERT not to be republished" on every page): open each page from its link.

CBSE names the three prescribed books (page 9); they are the units. Their lessons, from NCERT's contents pages, are the chapters.

Good to know:

- <b>Lessons from NCERT's books.</b> CBSE lists only the lessons it removes (page 9), and none of them is in the 2026-27 books, so every lesson on the contents pages is in. For अभिव्यक्ति और माध्यम, CBSE names the chapters Class XI uses: "पाठ संख्या 1, 2, 9, 10, 14, 15 तथा 16" (page 8); they are numbered 1–7 here.
- आरोह prints गद्य खंड (1–8) and then काव्य खंड (1–8); the poems follow as 9–16. <b>Please check:</b> अक्कमहादेवी's entry holds two poems, so the chapter is her name and the poems are its topics. The author column is not taken.
- Left out: वितान's "लेखकों के बारे में" and the footnote star after "आलो-आँधारि".
- Hindi Core is the <code>hindi</code> syllabus. Hindi Elective is a separate course and is not in the catalog.
- Each title is typed from the page as printed, in both the English and the Hindi column. Please compare each one with the page.
