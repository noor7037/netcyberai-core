ISC English is one subject with two papers: English Language (composition, directed writing, grammar, comprehension: question types, not chapters, so not taken) and Literature in English, read from three prescribed books. The units are the three books; the chapters are the short stories and poems listed for Class XII. The play is one unit with its acts in its name.

Good to know:

- The publisher printed in brackets after each book is left out of its name.
- Project work is not in the list.
