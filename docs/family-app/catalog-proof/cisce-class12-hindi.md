CISCE's Indian Languages syllabus has a language section (composition, comprehension, grammar: question types, not taken) and prescribed textbooks, listed for each language in Appendix I. The units are the four Hindi books; the chapters are the portion of the novel, the acts of the play, and the stories and poems listed for Class XII.

Good to know:

- <b>Please check:</b> CISCE asks for "Any three of the following books"; all four are in the list.
- CISCE prints these titles in Roman letters; they are copied as printed, and no Devanagari title is filled in.
- The publisher printed after each collection is left out of its name. The page prints two columns: read down the left column, then the right.
