The Class IX part has one heading, "PRINCIPLES OF GEOGRAPHY"; it is the unit. Its numbered topics are the chapters; the topics are the lettered items (a), (b) … under a chapter. The numbered items printed under a lettered item ((i), (ii) …) are not taken.

Good to know:

- <b>Please check:</b> "Hydrosphere" (page 5) prints three lines without a letter ("Meaning of hydrosphere.", "Tides - formation and pattern.", "Ocean Currents – …") and "Natural Regions of the World" (page 6) a statement; neither has topics.
- <b>Please check:</b> "Map Work" is in the list as a chapter, but its items (a)–(e) are lists of places to locate on an outline map, not topics, as for CBSE map work.
