The units are the course structure table's units (page 11). The chapters are the sub-headings printed under a unit in the syllabus ("(i) Inverse Trigonometric Functions"); a unit with no sub-heading has no chapter line. Each chapter is printed as running text, followed by an explanation in italics, so this version has no topics.

Good to know:

- Vector Algebra, Three - Dimensional Geometry, Linear Programming and Probability print no sub-heading, so they have no chapters.
- <b>Please check:</b> "Types of relations" is the name before the colon of item (i) under Relations and Functions ("Types of relations: reflexive, symmetric, …"). "Continuity, Differentiability and Differentiation" ends at the full stop where its content begins. Under Algebra, "Matrices and Determinants" is printed above (i) Matrices and (ii) Determinants and is read as the heading of that list, not as a chapter.
- The table's names are used: "Three - Dimensional Geometry" (text: "Three - dimensional Geometry"); the table prints "4 Vector Algebra" and "5 Three - Dimensional Geometry" without a full stop after the number.
- Project work is not in the list.
