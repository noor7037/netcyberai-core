Good to know:

- <b>Please check:</b> "Mensuration" and "Probability" (page 10) number no items, so they have no topics.
- <b>Please check:</b> Statistics (a) prints "Computation of:" and one bullet under it; the topic joins the two, up to the first full stop ("Computation of: Measures of Central Tendency: Mean, median, mode for raw and arrayed data.").
- Commercial Mathematics (ii) is "Banking"; the line under it ("Recurring Deposit Accounts: …") is its explanation and is not taken. Trigonometry (a) is taken without the identities printed under it.
