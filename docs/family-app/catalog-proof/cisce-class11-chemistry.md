The units are the course structure table's units (page 4). Chapters are taken only where a unit prints numbered sub-headings: Equilibrium and Hydrocarbons. The other units print their content as running text, so they have no chapter line, and this version has no topics (as for CBSE Chemistry).

Good to know:

- <b>Please check:</b> the table prints "Physical Chemistry", "Inorganic Chemistry" and "Organic Chemistry" in its weightage column, beside groups of units with their marks. They are not taken as a level.
- <b>Please check:</b> Chemical Thermodynamics prints numbered items (i)–(iv), but they are content ("Introduction, concepts, types of system, …"), not headings, so they are not chapters. Under Hydrocarbons the bold line "Classification of Hydrocarbons" is read as the heading of the numbered list below it (I. Aliphatic, II. Aromatic), not as a chapter.
- The table's names are used where the text words a unit differently: "Chemical Bonding and Molecular Structure" (text: "…structure"), "Organic Chemistry: Some basic Principles and Techniques" (text: "Organic Chemistry - Some Basic Principles and Techniques"). "Chemical Equilibrium." keeps its printed full stop.
- Practical work, project work and the practical file are not in the list.
