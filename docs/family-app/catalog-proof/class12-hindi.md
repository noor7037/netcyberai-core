No picture of CBSE's or NCERT's pages is kept in the repository (the documents are theirs, and NCERT prints "© NCERT not to be republished" on every page): open each page from its link.

CBSE names the three prescribed books (page 11); they are the units. Their lessons, from NCERT's contents pages, are the chapters.

Good to know:

- <b>Lessons from NCERT's books.</b> CBSE lists only the lessons it removes (page 11), and none of them is in the 2026-27 books, so every lesson on the contents pages is in. For अभिव्यक्ति और माध्यम, CBSE names the chapters Class XII uses: "पाठ संख्या 3, 4, 5, 11, 12 तथा 13" (page 10); they are numbered 1–6 here. <b>Please check:</b> question 4 on page 10 lists them without 12; the section heading's list is used.
- <b>Please check:</b> five entries of आरोह hold two works each; for them the chapter is the author's name as printed and the works are its topics. Every other chapter is the work's title; the author is not taken.
- The third book is printed in quotation marks (‘अभिव्यक्ति और माध्यम’); they are kept.
- Hindi Core is the <code>hindi</code> syllabus. Hindi Elective is a separate course and is not in the catalog.
- Each title is typed from the page as printed, in both the English and the Hindi column. Please compare each one with the page.
