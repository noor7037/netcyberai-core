The units are the course structure table's units (page 4). The chapters are the sub-headings printed under a unit in the syllabus ("(i) Motion in a Straight Line"); a unit with no sub-heading has no chapter line. Each chapter is printed as running text followed by an explanation in italics (partly as lettered paragraphs), not as a list, so this version has no topics.

Good to know:

- <b>Please check:</b> Laws of Motion; Work, Energy and Power; Motion of System of Particles and Rigid Body; and Gravitation print no sub-heading, so they have no chapters. The numbered paragraphs in italics under Work, Energy and Power and under Gravitation are explanation, not headings.
- Where the content follows a heading on the same line, the chapter is the name before the colon: "Thermal Properties of Matter", "Kinetic Theory", "Oscillations", "Waves". "Units and Measurements" is printed without a number under Physical World and Measurement.
- The table's name is used where the text words a unit differently: "Work, Energy and Power" (text: "Work, Power and Energy").
- Practical work, project work and the practical file are not in the list.
