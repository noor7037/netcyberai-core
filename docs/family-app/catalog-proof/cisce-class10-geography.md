The Class X part has two parts, "PART – I MAP WORK" and "PART - II GEOGRAPHY OF INDIA"; they are the units (the label "PART – I" is left out). The numbered topics are the chapters (CISCE numbers them 1 to 11 across the two parts; positions start again in each unit). The topics are the lettered items, or the bullets where a chapter has no letters; everything here is printed in italics, so each topic is the item up to its first full stop. The document is printed in two columns.

Good to know:

- <b>Please check:</b> "Map of India" is in the list as a chapter, but its content is lists of places to locate on an outline map ("Mountains, Peaks and Plateaus (To be marked): …"), not topics, as for CBSE map work. "Interpretation of Topographical Maps" lists skills, and its items (a)–(h) are topics.
- "Climate" prints one paragraph, so it has no topics. The statements printed before the bullets of "Manufacturing Industries" and "Transport" are not topics; the crop list under the last Agriculture bullet is not taken.
