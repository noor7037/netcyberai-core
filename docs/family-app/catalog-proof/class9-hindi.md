No picture of CBSE's or NCERT's pages is kept in the repository (the documents are theirs, and NCERT prints "© NCERT not to be republished" on every page): open each page from its link.

The first unit is खंड-ख, व्यावहारिक व्याकरण; its numbered items are the chapters; the items printed under a chapter are its topics. The second unit is NCERT's Class 9 Hindi book, गंगा; its twelve numbered lessons are the chapters.

Good to know:

- <b>Lessons from NCERT's book.</b> CBSE says literature is studied from NCERT's textbook under the 2023 framework (pages 5 and 10) but names neither the book nor its lessons. NCERT's textbook page lists one Class 9 Hindi book, गंगा, for R1 and R2 alike. Its title column gives the chapters; the author column is not taken. The two unnumbered "साथ-साथ पढ़ें" pieces and "भाषा संगम" are not lessons and are left out.
- <b>Please check:</b> the Class IX document has two schemes, आर-1 (R1, first language) and आर-2 (R2, second language). This list is R1. R2 has a different grammar list (page 9) and is not in the catalog yet.
- Each title is typed from the page exactly as printed, including its spacing ("शब्द -निर्माण", "सर्वनाम,विशेषण,क्रिया"), and is the same in the English and Hindi columns. Please compare each one with the page.
