The Class IX part numbers eight topics; they are the chapters (no units). The topics are the first numbered items under each chapter ((i), (ii) … or (a), (b) …), or its bullets where it numbers none; the words in upright type are taken, the explanation in italics is not. Lists printed under an item are not taken (the catalog has three levels).

Good to know:

- <b>Please check:</b> "The Periodic Table" (page 6) numbers no items; its four bullets are the topics. The statement printed above them ("Dobereiner’s Triads, …") is not a topic, nor is the statement before (i) in "Study of the First Element -Hydrogen".
- <b>Please check:</b> "Atmospheric and Environmental Chemistry" (page 8) prints two bold sub-headings without a number. The topics are (a)–(c) under "Atmospheric Pollution."; "Water Pollution." and the line under it are not in the list. They could be added.
- Water (iii) mixes upright and italic words in one line; the whole line is the topic. Chemical formulas are written with the subscripts printed (H₂SO₄).
