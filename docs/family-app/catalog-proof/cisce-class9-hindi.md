Hindi is one paper in two sections; the sections are the units ("SECTION A: LANGUAGE - 40 Marks", "SECTION B: PRESCRIBED TEXTS - 40 Marks", the label and the marks left out), from the Second Language document. The Language section names no topics. For the prescribed texts the document refers to Appendix I, which lists, for Class IX, (i) Short Stories, (ii) Poems, (iii) the novel and (iv) the one-act plays: they are the chapters, and the lessons under them are the topics.

Good to know:

- <b>Please check:</b> CISCE prints the Hindi lessons in Roman letters ("Baat Athanni Ki - Sudarshan"), not in Devanagari. They are copied as printed; no Hindi title is filled in, and nothing was transliterated or translated.
- Appendix I says "Only two of the following books are to be offered": a student studies two of Sahitya Sagar (the short stories and poems), Naya Raasta (the novel) and Ekanki Sanchay (the plays). All are in the list. The instruction "(The following short stories are to be studied)" is left out of the chapter name.
- The Language section is not split between the classes; the prescribed texts are, for Hindi, by CISCE's circular of 20 April 2026 ("Bifurcation of the Prescribed Texts for Selected Indian Languages …").
