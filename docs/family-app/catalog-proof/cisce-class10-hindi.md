Hindi is one paper in two sections; the sections are the units, from the Second Language document. The Language section names no topics. For the prescribed texts the document refers to Appendix I, which lists, for Class X, (i) Short Stories, (ii) Poems, (iii) the novel and (iv) the one-act plays: they are the chapters, and the lessons under them are the topics.

Good to know:

- <b>Please check:</b> CISCE prints the Hindi lessons in Roman letters, not in Devanagari. They are copied as printed; no Hindi title is filled in, and nothing was transliterated or translated.
- Appendix I says "Only two of the following books are to be offered": a student studies two of Sahitya Sagar (the short stories and poems), Naya Raasta (the novel) and Ekanki Sanchay (the plays). All are in the list.
