ISC English is one subject with two papers: English Language (composition, directed writing, grammar, comprehension: question types, not chapters, so not taken) and Literature in English, read from three prescribed books. The units are the three books; the chapters are the short stories and poems listed for Class XI. The play is one unit with its acts in its name.

Good to know:

- The publisher printed in brackets after each anthology ("(Evergreen Publications (India) Ltd, New Delhi)") is left out of its name.
- "Abhisara:The Tryst – Rabindranath Tagore" is kept as printed, with no space after the colon.
- The English syllabus (page 9) names only the three books; the stories and poems are listed in this Appendix. Project work is not in the list.
