No picture of CBSE's or NCERT's pages is kept in the repository (the documents are theirs, and NCERT prints "© NCERT not to be republished" on every page): open each page from its link.

The first unit is खंड-ख, व्यावहारिक व्याकरण; its numbered items are the chapters; the bracketed list after अलंकार is that chapter's one topic. The other two units are the books CBSE prescribes (page 11); their lessons, from NCERT's contents pages, are the chapters.

Good to know:

- <b>Lessons from NCERT's books.</b> Page 11 names the two books and the lessons from which no question is asked, not the lessons studied, so they come from each book's contents page. None of the seven lessons on page 11 is in the 2026-27 books, so every lesson on the contents pages is in.
- <b>Please check:</b> क्षितिज numbers each author with the works under them. An entry with one work is that work's title; an entry with several (सूरदास, सूर्यकांत त्रिपाठी ‘निराला’, नागार्जुन) is the author's name, and each work is a topic (सूरदास's four पद by their first lines). कृतिका prints title and author; the author is not taken, and "लेखक-परिचय" is left out.
- Hindi Course B (code 085) is a separate course and is not in the catalog yet.
- Each title is typed from the page exactly as printed and is the same in the English and Hindi columns. Please compare each one with the page.
