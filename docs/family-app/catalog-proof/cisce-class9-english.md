English is one subject in two papers; the papers are the units ("PAPER 1 - ENGLISH LANGUAGE", "PAPER 2 - LITERATURE IN ENGLISH", the label "PAPER 1 -" left out). Under "SYLLABUS TO BE COVERED", Class IX, the two prescribed texts are the chapters and the stories and poems of Treasure Chest are its topics: the five stories, then the five poems (the sub-headings "PROSE (Short Stories):" and "POETRY:" are not lines).

Good to know:

- <b>Please check:</b> the English Language unit has no chapters. Paper 1 describes its five questions (composition, letter, notice and e-mail, comprehension, grammar): an exam format, not a list of topics.
- Titles are copied with their authors, as printed. CISCE's Appendix I (list of prescribed books) prints one title differently ("The Home-coming"); the syllabus document's "Home-coming" is used.
