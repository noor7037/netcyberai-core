Good to know:

- <b>Please check:</b> "Motion in One Dimension" (page 4) numbers no items: it prints one statement ("Scalar and vector quantities, distance, speed, …") and its explanation, so it has no topics.
- Heat and Energy (ii) is printed in italics throughout; its topic is its first sentence. The degree sign, printed as a raised 0, is written "°C".
- Electricity and Magnetism (i) is longer than a title may be (300 characters), so the topic is its first part, up to the first semicolon.
- A semicolon at the end of an item is dropped (Fluids (i)). The note on SI units and the practical work are not in the list.
