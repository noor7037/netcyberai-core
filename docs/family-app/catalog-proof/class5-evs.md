Units and chapters are the contents page's lines. Topics are the section headings printed inside each chapter: a topic's page link opens the chapter's own PDF at that page.

Topics are the dark red bold headings, in printed order. Not taken: the “Let us reflect” and “Let us practice” exercise labels, and the smaller pink and blue headings inside chapter 3 (for example “The Forgotten Tiffin Box!” and “Food Preservation”).

Each line shows its Hindi title under the English one, with a link that opens the Hindi PDF at the page it is printed on. The Hindi chapters print the same dark red headings in the same order, so each English heading took the Hindi heading in the same place: the same page and the same content. NCERT's Hindi headings are its own wording, not word-for-word translations (for example “Food in Our Body” is “हमारे शरीर में भोजन की यात्रा”), and some English headings end in “!” where the Hindi does not. Three things to know:

- **Please check “Fire Safety”** (chapter 4, page 14). The Hindi edition prints “अग्नि सुरक्षा” as the title of the green box below it, not as a dark red heading; it is the same section, so it was taken.
- **“One Earth, One Family!”** (chapter 10) is on page 13 in English and page 14 in Hindi: the Hindi text runs longer.
- **Two Hindi headings are not on our list:** “परिवर्तनों का अवलोकन” (chapter 1, page 6) and “खाद्य संरक्षण” (chapter 3, page 6) are dark red headings in Hindi, but the English edition prints them as smaller headings (“Observing Changes”, “Food Preservation”), which are not topics.
