Unit 1 is the "II Grammar" heading with its bullets as chapters. Units 2 and 3 are the two NCERT books on page 4, with their lessons and poems as chapters.

Good to know:

- CBSE also has a second Class X English course, "English Communicative" (code 101). It is not in this list: it names no chapters, and Class IX English continues as Language and Literature.
- FIRST FLIGHT prints prose 1–9, then poems 1–10. Our list numbers them 1–19 in the same order: "Dust of Snow" (poem 1) is chapter 10.
- The workbook (WORDS AND EXPRESSIONS – II, "Units 1 to 4 and Units 7 to 11") has no lines: CBSE does not print the unit names.
- Reading passages, writing tasks and question formats are not in the list: they are exam formats, not chapters.
