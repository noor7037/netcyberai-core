The Class X part has two sections, Civics and History; they are the units. The numbered topics of each section are the chapters; the topics are the first numbered items under a chapter, in the words printed in upright type, before the explanation CISCE prints in italics. The document is printed in two columns.

Good to know:

- The Union Legislature (i) and (ii) are printed in italics throughout; each topic is the item up to its first full stop. The unnumbered line "Meaning of the federal setup in India." is not a topic.
- <b>Please check:</b> History items name the topic in upright type and continue in italics after a dash ("Factors leading to the growth of Nationalism – economic exploitation, …"); the upright words are the topic. "Foundation of the Indian National Congress" (under (b)) and "Second Phase of the Indian National Movement (1905-1916)" (under (c)) are printed without a letter and are not separate topics.
- "Forward Bloc (objectives) and INA (name of the founder objectives and contribution of Subhas Chandra Bose)." is kept whole: the words in brackets are in italics inside one line. The two items under "United Nations" are not taken.
- The chapter "Mass Phase of the National Movement (1915-1947)" is printed across a line break ("(1915-" / "1947)"); the two parts are joined.
