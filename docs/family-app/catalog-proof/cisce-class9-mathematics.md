The Class IX part numbers eight topics (Pure Arithmetic, Commercial Mathematics, Algebra …); they are the chapters (no units). The topics are the first numbered items under each chapter ((i) … or (a) …); lettered items under an (i) item are not taken. An item printed entirely in italics is taken up to its first full stop.

Good to know:

- <b>Please check:</b> "Pure Arithmetic" (page 4) numbers no items, so it has no topics: "Rational and Irrational Numbers" and "Proofs of irrationality of √2, √3, √5" are printed without a number. In "Commercial Mathematics" the unnumbered heading "Compound Interest" is not a topic; its items (a) and (b) are.
- The formula in Commercial Mathematics (b) is written in plain characters: "A = P(1 + r/100)ⁿ".
- The statement printed before (a) in Statistics, Mensuration and Coordinate Geometry is not a topic. A colon at the end of an item is dropped.
