Good to know:

- <b>Please check:</b> under "Morphology of Flowering Plants" the syllabus prints two lettered items, "(a) Morphology and modifications of root, stem, leaf." and "(b) Morphology of flower. …". They are not taken as topics, so that the whole subject follows one rule (no topics, as for CBSE Biology).
- Three Human Physiology headings end with a full stop as printed ("Breathing and exchange of gases.").
- Practical work, project work, the practical file and the list of scientists are not in the list.
