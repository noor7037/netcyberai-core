The units are the course structure table's seven units (page 9). The chapters of Partnership Accounts and Joint Stock Company Accounts are the parts the table lists under them (A., B., C. …); the other units' chapters are the first-level items in the text ("(i) …", or "A. Liquidity Ratios:" … in Ratio Analysis). The topics are the first-level items under a chapter: the table's I., II., III. under Reconstitution of Partnership, and the text's items under the other chapters.

Good to know:

- <b>Please check:</b> the paper is printed in Sections A, B and C, and a candidate answers Section B (units 3–5, Management Accounting) or Section C (units 6–7, Computerised Accounting). Both options are in the list; the sections are not taken as a level.
- Financial Statement Analysis (a unit) has no chapters and Final Accounts of Companies (a chapter) has no topics: both print running text. The ratio names end with a colon as printed ("Current Ratio:").
- Not taken: the notes ("NOTE:"), the "Amendments" under Final Accounts of Companies, the bullet lists under a label in Cash Flow Statement and DBMS ("The following items are to be taken when …", "The following examples of DBMS …"), and the lists under a topic (the items under Admission, Retirement and Death of a Partner, and Dissolution).
- Project work is not in the list. The pages print two columns: read down the left column, then the right.
