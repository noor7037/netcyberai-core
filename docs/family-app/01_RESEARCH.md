## 2. Pain points, ranked by how often they hit and how much they cost the family

| # | Pain | Who | Evidence | What the app does about it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | "My child is in Class 6 on paper but cannot divide." Gaps from earlier classes stay invisible until an exam. | Student, parent | E2 | A short foundation check per subject, then practice pinned to the missing skill, shown to the parent in plain words. |
| P2 | The parent cannot help with homework: the language, the method, or time. | Parent | E5, E3 | Topic explainers in the family's language with the English term beside it; method-first homework help; for linked schools, "what was taught today and how to help at home". |
| P3 | Revision has no plan; the exam arrives and everything is urgent. Anxiety follows. | Student | E4 | A day-by-day plan to the exam date that reflows from today when a day is missed, never a backlog of failures. |
| P4 | Coaching is the default fix, and it is expensive. | Parent | E3 | Daily practice and doubt help at a price well under coaching; the app never claims to replace a teacher. |
| P5 | Scholarships and exams are missed because nobody told the family in time. | Parent, student | E6 | A verified list matched to the child's class, state and category, each with the official link, the date it was checked, and a reminder before the last date. |

## 3. What the market tells us to avoid

- **Selling on fear** (E8): no "your child will fall behind" copy, no counsellor calls, no EMI.
- **Competing on content volume:** free government content (DIKSHA) and free AI tutors (E9) already exist. The
  difference is context: the child's syllabus, the school's day, the family's language, and the parent's view.
- **Reproducing NCERT books** (E11): link to the official chapter; never copy or adapt the text, in any tier.
- **Promising "no distractions" with YouTube** (E13): a curated video may end with more videos from the same channel.
  Say so, or use only channels that have nothing else public.
- **Tracking children for engagement** (E12): no streak pressure that punishes, no analytics trackers on child screens,
  no ads. Whether adaptive practice counts as "behavioural monitoring" for a non-school company is a question for the
  lawyer before the standalone mode opens to the public (`02` §7, gate L2).

## 4. Questions for every pilot school's parents (the founder asks; answers go in the Decision Log)

1. What did you pay for tuition or coaching this year, per child?
2. Last week, what homework could you not help with, and why?
3. Which scholarship or exam did your child miss because you heard too late?
4. Which school message did you miss in a WhatsApp group this month?
5. Would you pay for daily practice and doubt help, and how much a month for all your children together?
