## 3. What the market tells us to avoid

- **Selling on fear** (E8): no "your child will fall behind" copy, no counsellor calls, no EMI.
- **Competing on content volume:** free government content (DIKSHA) and free AI tutors (E9) already exist. The
  difference is context: the child's syllabus, the school's day, the family's language, and the parent's view.
- **Reproducing NCERT books** (E11): link to the official chapter; never copy or adapt the text, in any tier.
- **Promising "no distractions" with YouTube** (E13): a curated video may end with more videos from the same channel.
  Say so, or use only channels that have nothing else public.
- **Tracking children for engagement** (E12): no streak pressure that punishes, no analytics trackers on child screens,
  no ads. Whether adaptive practice counts as "behavioural monitoring" for a non-school company is a question for the
  lawyer before the standalone mode opens to the public (`02` §14, gate L2).

## 4. Questions for every pilot school's parents (the founder asks; answers go in the Decision Log)

1. What did you pay for tuition or coaching this year, per child?
2. Last week, what homework could you not help with, and why?
3. Which scholarship or exam did your child miss because you heard too late?
4. Which school message did you miss in a WhatsApp group this month?
5. Would you pay for daily practice and doubt help, and how much a month for all your children together?

## 5. Competitors and what NetCyberAI should do differently (2026-10-20)

| Product | What it does well | What it lacks for our families |
|---|---|---|
| Khanmigo | Socratic tutoring that does not hand out answers; writing coach | Built for the US curriculum; parent plan in the US only (E10) |
| Gemini Guided Learning, ChatGPT Study Mode | Free, multimodal, guides step by step (E9) | No knowledge of the child's syllabus, school day or past mistakes; no parent view; open chat |
| Duolingo Max | Conversation practice with feedback; explanations of each answer (E24) | General English, not tied to school subjects or careers; priced for wealthier markets |
| PhysicsWallah | Low price, exam focus, huge video library (E10) | Content, not a personal loop; no school link |

**The differentiation that holds up:** one coherent, syllabus-aware loop in the family's language that links
understanding → checked practice → mastery → exams → English → career choices → opportunities, with the parent able
to see it, and the child's school day feeding it when the school is on NetCyberAI.
