**Full description** (max 4000). The first paragraph is the monitoring disclosure Google's rule asks for and stays first:

```
IMPORTANT: NetCyberAI Focus is a parental control app for parents to use on a phone their own child uses. It watches which app is in front on this phone, so it can close the apps the parent has chosen during the study times the parent has set. It shows a permanent "Focus Lock is on" notification while it works, and it does not hide itself. What the app sees about app use stays on this phone and is never sent to us or to anyone. Use it only for your own children, and tell your child it is on.

How it works
- You (the parent) install NetCyberAI Focus and connect it from the NetCyberAI Family app, in the parent area, with one tap.
- You choose the entertainment apps to lock (for example games, video, social media and browsers) and the study times, such as weekdays 4 pm to 9 pm.
- When your child opens a locked app during those times, a "Study first" screen appears. It lists what is left to study today and a button to start.
- When today's study in the NetCyberAI Family app is finished, the chosen apps open for the rest of that lock window.
- Calls, messages and emergency calls are never locked. You choose whether WhatsApp stays open. Educational apps you mark stay open.
- Your 6-digit parent PIN opens a short parent session. The lock comes back by itself after the time you set, or when the screen turns off.
- If a permission is switched off, the phone's clock is changed, or the lock stops reporting, you are told in the parent area of the NetCyberAI Family app.

What the app needs, and why
- Usage access: to know which app is in front, on the phone only.
- Display over other apps: to show the "Study first" screen over a locked app.
- Notifications: for the permanent "Focus Lock is on" notice.
- Internet: to read whether today's study is done and what is pending, and to send three yes or no facts about the lock (see below).
It does not use the Accessibility service, it is not a device administrator, and it does not read your child's messages, photos, location, browsing or calls.

What leaves the phone
- It reads from our server: whether today's study is done and the titles of what is pending.
- About every 30 minutes while the lock is on it sends three facts only: the lock is on, its permissions are on, the phone's clock was changed.
- Nothing about which app was opened, or when, is ever sent. No advertisements. We do not sell data.
- The parent PIN is kept on the phone only as a salted hash.

Honest limits
- No ordinary app can be made impossible to remove. A child can still reset the phone or use safe mode. NetCyberAI Focus makes this hard and tells you when the lock stops.
- Some phone brands stop background apps. The app guides you through the brand's battery and autostart settings.

For parents of children only. Not for use on another adult's phone, and not for secret monitoring. Needs the NetCyberAI Family app and a parent account.

Questions: admin@netcyberai.com
```
