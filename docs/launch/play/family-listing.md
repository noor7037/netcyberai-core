## English

**Short description** (max 80):

```
AI study help for Class 1 to 12 children, with a parent area and quizzes
```

**Full description** (max 4000):

```
NetCyberAI Family helps a child understand school lessons and practise until it makes sense. A parent signs in once, adds the child, and the child can learn on the same phone.

What a child can do
- Ask the AI tutor a question in simple words, in English, Hindi or Hinglish. Answers are made by AI and can be wrong, so check what matters with your book or your teacher.
- Read chapters for their class and study with explanations, worked examples, diagrams and practice.
- Practise English with reading aloud and speaking, using the phone's microphone only when the child taps a microphone button.
- Take quizzes and chapter tests. Answers are checked, and progress is saved for the parent to see.
- Take a photo of a textbook page to get help with it.

What a parent can do
- Sign in with an email code. There is no password.
- Add children, choose their class and language, and see their checked answers and progress.
- Keep the parent area behind a 6-digit parent PIN, so a child on a shared phone cannot open it.
- Delete a child's answers, or delete the whole account, from the parent area.
- Link the child's school (School Link) to see attendance, homework, results and fees from the school.
- Connect the separate NetCyberAI Focus app, if you want entertainment apps to stay closed until the day's study is done. That is a different app with its own Play listing.

Safety and privacy
- No advertisements. We do not sell data.
- A child's name, the parent's email and ID numbers are removed before anything is sent to the AI.
- A safety check reads every question and every part of an answer before it is shown.
- Photos and recordings are read once and are not stored.
- You can read how the AI works inside the app, and our privacy policy at netcyberai.com/privacy.

NetCyberAI Family is a learning app. It does not replace a teacher. It is not endorsed by any education board.

Questions: admin@netcyberai.com
```
