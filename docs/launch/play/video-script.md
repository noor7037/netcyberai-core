# Foreground service video: 30-second script

**For:** Noor. **Delivers:** the screen recording Play asks for with the foreground service declaration (`forms.md` section 10). **How:** Android's built-in screen recorder (swipe down twice, tap Screen record, then Start). No voice-over needed; Play only needs to see the service doing its job.

## Before you press record (once)

1. Install both apps from the same build (Family, then Focus), see `docs/launch/FOCUS_TEST.md`.
2. Finish the Focus set-up once so every step is ticked. Set the lock days and times to include now.
3. Make sure today's study in the Family app is **not** finished, so the block screen shows.
4. Stop the lock (so the video starts from "off"), and put YouTube or any game on the home screen.

## The 30 seconds, in order

| Seconds | What you do | What Play sees |
|---|---|---|
| 0-4 | Open NetCyberAI Focus from the home screen | The app, with the lock off |
| 4-8 | Tap **Start** | The lock turns on |
| 8-12 | Swipe down from the top of the screen | The permanent "Focus Lock is on" notification |
| 12-14 | Swipe the notification shade away, press Home | The home screen |
| 14-20 | Open YouTube (or the chosen game) | The "Study first" screen over it, listing what is left and the Start studying button |
| 20-23 | Press Back | The home screen again (the blocked app is not shown) |
| 23-27 | Open Phone (the dialler) | It opens normally: calls are never locked |
| 27-30 | Swipe down again | The "Focus Lock is on" notification is still there. Stop recording |

## After recording

1. Stop the recorder (swipe down, tap the red recording bar).
2. Upload the video to YouTube as **unlisted**, or to Drive with link sharing on, and paste the link in the declaration form (`[GAP: video link]`).
3. Do not show your parent PIN, your email or your child's name on screen. Re-record if any shows.
