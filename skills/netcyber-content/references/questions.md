# Questions: checks, practice and the chapter test

Questions are for learning first and certification second. Every option teaches.

## Items (format: `reader/CONTRACT.md` §4)

| Type | Use it for | Write it like this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `mcq` | one idea, one decision | Stem asks one thing. 4 options of similar length and form. The key is unambiguous. Each distractor is a real mistake. |
| `ar` | cause and reason (Classes 9–12) | Assertion and Reason are each a full statement; vary the key across 0–3 in the chapter (not always "both true, R explains A"). |
| `case` | competency: a situation, data table, source or observation + 2–4 MCQ parts | The stem gives real-looking data or a scene; each part needs the stem; mark one or two parts at "analyse". |
| `short` (2–3 marks) | explain, compare, a 2-step numerical | `model` answer in board style; `rubric` one point per mark, each checkable. |
| `long` (5 marks) | derive, design, explain a process fully | `model` complete; 5 rubric points. |

`mcq` with `"comp": true` only when it is genuinely data/source-based.

## The `why` for every option

- Right option: why it is right, in one or two sentences, naming the idea.
- Wrong option: the thinking that leads there and the correction — "This adds the denominators too; only the
  numerators add when the denominators match." Never "Wrong." Never about the child.

## Distractor bank (start from these; add the chapter's own)

- Maths: sign error; squaring a sum; order of operations; area ↔ perimeter; dividing by something that may be 0;
  confusing a theorem and its converse; misreading a graph's scale.
- Science: heat ≠ temperature; current "used up" in a bulb; heavier falls faster; rusting is "just mixing"; plants
  "eat" soil; photosynthesis only by day in green leaves; the image always forms at F; colour decides scattering.
- Social science: one festival/food = "Indian culture"; development = cities only; maps are pictures, not models.

## Topic pool (8 items, 5 drawn, 4 to pass)

Mix: 5–6 `mcq`, 1–2 `ar` or `case` (Classes 9–12). Cover the topic's outcomes, not one fact eight times. Bloom spread per
`pedagogy.md`. Spread correct positions (the checker counts the whole chapter: no index > 35%).

## Chapter test (Classes 9–12): 20 items, CBSE Acad-30/2024 by marks

| Share by marks | Items |
| --- | --- |
| Competency 45–55% | 4–5 `case` (3 parts each), 3–4 `ar`, data-based `mcq` (`comp`) |
| Plain MCQ 15–25% | 6–7 `mcq` |
| Constructed 25–35% | 4 `short` (mostly 2 marks), 1 `long` (5 marks) |

A typical working total is 38 marks: 19 / 6 / 13. Interleave topics; every topic appears; include at least one
numerical (maths/physics/chemistry), one diagram-based item, one real-life application. Pass mark 0.7.

## Practice steps

- `retrieve`: one quick item on an earlier topic or class (spaced recall).
- `guided`: an item one step harder than the worked example, with a `hint` that points to the idea, not the answer.
- `worked.faded.item`: the faded example's missing step as an MCQ.
