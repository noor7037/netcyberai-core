# Tutor visual sources: free for commercial use only (Noor, 16 Nov 2026)

NetCyberAI is a paid product (₹299/year), so use ONLY sources whose licence allows commercial use. Before wiring a source, VERIFY its licence, its endpoint and its rate limit from the official docs, and record them in config. Drop any source that turns out to be non-commercial. Never use: GeoGebra, CK-12, Desmos production API, Freepik/Storyset, Thingiverse, Free3D, or any NC (non-commercial) licence.

Allowed licences: public domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, MIT, Apache, and the free-commercial licences of Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay, Mixkit, Coverr and Lottie Simple.
- Students see only the visual; no credit is shown on screen.
- For CC BY and CC BY-SA, store author, source and licence in metadata, and list them on one "Picture credits" page (needed to stay legal).

## Access modes
- LIVE: searched at answer time through the server (never the phone), cached in content.image_picks.
- BULK: downloaded once by a script into our own assets (labs/showcase/models), with metadata, then served from our storage.
